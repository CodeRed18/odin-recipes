Description:

A classic Italian dessert featuring layers of coffee-dipped ladyfingers and a rich, whipped mixture of mascarpone cheese, eggs, and sugar, all topped with a dusting of cocoa powder

Ingredients:

  • Ladyfingers
  • Mascarpone
  • Cheese
  • Eggs
  • Sugar
  • Coffee

Steps:

  1. Mix creamy filling.
  2. Dip lady fingers.
  3. Layer mascarpone.
  4. Repeat.

Note:

Make 2-3 days in advance of any events, due to time needed in the refrigerator
