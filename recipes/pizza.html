Description:

An Italian dish of a flattened bread dough topped with sauce, cheese, and other ingredients, which is then baked at a high temperature.

Ingredients:

  • Dough made from (flour, water, yeast, and salt)
  • A tomato-based sauce
  • Cheese, typically mozzarella
  • Toppings

Steps:

  1. Mix and Proof.
  2. Ball the Dough.
  3. Proof Dough in Cast Iron Pan.
  4. Touch Up the Dough.
  5. Sauce the Dough.
  6. Add Toppings.
  7. Bake in a Hot Oven.
  8. Add Some Post-Bake Flair.
