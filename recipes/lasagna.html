Description:

Lasagna is a traditional Italian comfort food made by layering wide, flat pasta sheets with rich tomato or meat sauce, creamy béchamel or ricotta, and generous amounts of melted cheese, typically mozzarella and Parmesan. Baked until golden and bubbling, it offers a delicious balance of flavors and textures—savory, creamy, and slightly crisp on top—making it a beloved dish enjoyed around the world in countless variations.

Ingredients:

  • Meat Sauce (Ragù or Bolognese)
  • Béchamel Sauce (White Sauce)
  • Ricotta Mixture (common in American-style lasagna)
  • Pasta Sheets
  • Cheese Layer

Steps:

  • Layer in a baking dish:
  1. Meat sauce
  2. Pasta sheets
  3. Ricotta mixture or béchamel
  4. Mozzarella + Parmesan
  • (Repeat layers and finish with cheese on top.)
  • Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 40–50 minutes until golden and bubbling.
  • Let rest for 10–15 minutes before serving.
